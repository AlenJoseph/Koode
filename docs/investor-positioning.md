# Investor positioning — Koode vs ChatGPT

## One-liner

**Koode is a learning companion closely integrated with the student** — pathways, struggle, help, and evidence — so they learn easily *and* build judgement for the AI age. It is **not** ChatGPT with a school skin.

## The problem ChatGPT does not solve

Students already have infinite answers. They lack:

1. A **path** (what to practise next)
2. **Productive struggle** with a guide who will not steal the work
3. **Proof** they can do it without the model
4. Habits to **use AI wisely** (verify, question, create)

ChatGPT optimises for the next message. Koode optimises for the next capable human.

## Closely integrated — what we mean

| Layer | Meaning | Product signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In the moment | Knows the step, recent fails, flags, idle | Learning events + auto-help + Copilot |
| Across the journey | Pathways, checkpoints, resume | Lesson player + enrollments |
| Around the learner | Teacher support view, not clinical labels | Classroom insights |
| After the work | Evidence artifacts; rewards only on success | Certificates + Koode coins |

Integration is **stateful and pedagogical**, not “remembers chat history.”

## Prepare for the AI age

Definition we sell:

> The learner can pose problems, verify answers, build things they understand, and choose when AI helps versus when they must think alone.

Koode trains that by design: Socratic help, teach-back, reflection, projects, evidence criteria. AI is a **tool, not an authority** (product principle #3).

## Easy to learn (without dumbing down)

Ease = clear next step, multi-modal lessons, pace/local adaptation, soft stuck nudges.  
Hard thinking stays with the learner. Copilot does not hand over quiz keys.

## Category & moat (diligence)

- **Category:** Adolescent learning OS (curriculum loop + companion AI + evidence), not consumer chat.
- **Moat:** Longitudinal learner activity + classroom workflow + portable evidence. Chat UIs are commodity.
- **Refuse:** Auto-complete work, sell certs for coins, permanent ability scores, infra theater (Kafka before product).

## Honest status

**Show now:** companion loop on showcase pathway (lesson → stuck help → evidence/coins).  
**Not yet:** school-grade email/deploy, dense multi-pathway lessons, full graph Learner Model.

Pitch the destination; demo the loop; do not claim ChatGPT replacement.

## Pitch close

> We’re not building a better chat. We’re building the system that stays with the student while they become someone who doesn’t need to hide behind one.
